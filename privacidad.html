Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

QuantixBuilds (“we”) operates the construction estimating platform at quantixbuilds.com. This policy explains how we handle information from people who create an account or use the service.

Information we collect

We collect your name and email address solely to provide our construction estimating service and manage your account. We may also store data needed to operate that account, such as the selected plan and free-trial dates.

How we use information

We use this data to create and maintain your account, sign you in, provide estimate, invoice, and contract tools, and send service-related messages (for example, password recovery).

We do not sell or share data

QuantixBuilds will never sell or share your data with third parties. We do not sell contact lists or provide your information for other companies’ advertising.

Retention and security

We keep information while your account is active or as needed to provide the service. We apply reasonable measures to protect it against unauthorized access.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us through the channels published at quantixbuilds.com.
